# Tokenomics and supply policy

## Enforced by the contract

- Fixed supply: 1,000,000,000 XIAO.
- Decimals: 18.
- Supply is minted once to the treasury at deployment.
- No function can increase supply afterward.
- Standard transfers move balances but do not change total supply.

## Not enforced by the contract

The contract does not enforce allocation percentages, vesting schedules,
liquidity commitments, governance, prices, redemption, revenue sharing, or
community rewards. Any such statement would require separate contracts,
operational controls, legal review, and public documentation.

| Advisors and collaborators | 5% | 50,000,000 XIAO |

Before adopting any allocation:

1. define a legitimate product use;
2. obtain legal and tax advice for every relevant jurisdiction;
3. document vesting and conflicts of interest;
4. use transparent multisignature and vesting contracts;
5. publish wallet labels and periodic treasury reports;
6. avoid statements that promise profit, price support, or guaranteed liquidity.

## Stablecoin distinction

XIAO is not designed to maintain a fixed value relative to USD or another asset.
No reserve, redemption promise, or peg mechanism exists in this repository.
