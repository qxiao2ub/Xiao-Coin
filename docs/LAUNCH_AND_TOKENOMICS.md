# Launch model and tokenomics

## Official launch

Xiao-Coin was created through Base.meme on Base Mainnet using the Standard launch
flow shown by the project creator. The official contract is:

`0xc62792b29E6aDbc179e47DAfCe159119bb918888`

## Base.meme Standard model

According to Base.meme's current fair-launch documentation:

- total supply: 1,000,000,000 tokens;
- bonding-curve allocation: 800,000,000 tokens (80%);
- liquidity-pool reserve: 200,000,000 tokens (20%);
- no automatic creator/team allocation in the Standard model;
- initial price discovery occurs on a bonding curve;
- after graduation, liquidity is migrated to a Uniswap market and locked under
  the platform's process.

Xiao-Coin's launch selected ETH as the pair / raised token and displayed a 2.5 ETH
raising goal. The project page identifies the launch as a V4 market path and showed
0% token tax at launch.

## What is not fixed by this repository

The following are dynamic and should be read from the live Base.meme or BaseScan page:

- price and market capitalization;
- bonding-curve progress;
- remaining curve supply;
- volume, holder count, and creator balance;
- accumulated collateral and rewards;
- graduation timing and trading liquidity.

## Creator allocation and holdings

Standard-mode creation does not automatically give the creator free XIAO. The
creator may purchase tokens through the same launch mechanism or receive transfers.
This repository does not state a current creator balance because holdings are dynamic.

## Creator rewards

Base.meme currently documents creator rewards based on eligible trading activity.
At the time this repository was prepared, its documentation described 1% of trade
value during the bonding-curve phase and 1% for V4 market trading after graduation.
This is a platform rule, not guaranteed income. Check current documentation and the
actual reward recipient before relying on it.

## No price or liquidity promise

Fixed supply, a bonding curve, a market-cap display, or locked liquidity does not
ensure demand, profit, an exit price, or the ability to sell a large position without
slippage. Market value may fall to zero.
