# How to buy or sell Xiao-Coin

This is an operational overview, not a recommendation to trade.

## Before interacting

1. Use the official page: https://base.meme/coin/base:0xc62792b29E6aDbc179e47DAfCe159119bb918888
2. Confirm the wallet network is **Base Mainnet (chain ID 8453)**.
3. Compare the full contract address with `0xc62792b29E6aDbc179e47DAfCe159119bb918888`.
4. Keep enough Base ETH for gas.
5. Review the amount you will receive, fees, slippage, and wallet simulation.

## Buy through Base.meme

1. Open the official Xiao-Coin page.
2. Connect a wallet you control.
3. Select **Buy**.
4. Enter the ETH amount.
5. Review the quoted XIAO amount and all fees.
6. Confirm only if the network, domain, contract, and amount are correct.
7. Verify the confirmed transaction on BaseScan.

## Sell through Base.meme

1. Open the same official page and select **Sell**.
2. Enter the XIAO amount or percentage.
3. Review expected ETH, price impact, platform fee, slippage, and gas.
4. Sign only the intended transaction.
5. Verify the receipt on BaseScan.

## Important limitations

A displayed market price is a marginal quote. Selling a large amount may move the
curve or pool price and return substantially less than a simple balance-times-price
calculation. A transaction can also fail or be front-run. Never spend funds you
cannot afford to lose.
