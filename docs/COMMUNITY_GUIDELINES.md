# Xiao-Coin community guidelines

The Xiao-Coin community is intended to center creativity, humor, internet culture,
and voluntary participation.

## Encourage

- original memes, art, translations, and educational explanations;
- transparent disclosure of conflicts, sponsorships, and creator holdings;
- respectful debate and independent verification;
- security warnings and responsible bug reports;
- realistic discussion of risk and uncertainty.

## Prohibit

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or doxxing;
- guaranteed-profit claims or pressure to buy;
- impersonation and counterfeit contract addresses;
- wash trading, fake volume, fake engagement, and coordinated manipulation;
- seed-phrase requests, phishing links, or malicious wallet instructions;
- unauthorized claims of Base, Coinbase, Base.meme, or Uniswap affiliation.
